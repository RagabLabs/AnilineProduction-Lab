Reviewed 2026-10-16. External references supplement the supplied lecture; no external kinetics or empirical selectivity values are used.

## Atomic weights and independent formula checks

1. CIAAW. [Abridged Standard Atomic Weights 2024](https://www.ciaaw.org/abridged-atomic-weights.htm). Source of H, C, N, O and S values used throughout the engine. Molecular masses are derived by formula summation.
2. NIST Chemistry WebBook, SRD 69. [Benzene, CAS 71-43-2](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C71432), [nitrobenzene, CAS 98-95-3](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C98953), [aniline, CAS 62-53-3](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C62533), [cyclohexylamine, CAS 108-91-8](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C108918). Formula/identity checks; their separately rounded molecular masses are not mixed with CIAAW-derived values.

## DVS convention

*Continuous flow nitration in miniaturized devices*. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry (2014), 10, 405–424. [doi:10.3762/bjoc.10.38](https://www.beilstein-journals.org/bjoc/articles/10/38). Used for the sulfuric-acid/final-water mass-ratio convention. The lecture's printed reaction-water denominator is also retained and its discrepancy is explained. The cited article is not used as a kinetic calibration.

## Declared selectivity extension

*Toward High Selectivity Aniline Synthesis Catalysis at Elevated Temperatures*. Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research (2021), 60, 17917 onward. [doi:10.1021/acs.iecr.1c03695](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.iecr.1c03695). [Accessible author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8802303/). Primary research discussing overhydrogenation and additional product pathways. This app introduces an explicitly restricted two-product AN/CHA network to make selectivity and mass closure computable. It does not claim this restricted network represents every industrial catalyst.

The following are application modeling choices, not claims transcribed from the lecture: pure-species internal kmol/kg basis; the selected AN/CHA network; fixed component splitter fractions; nitrogen as the recycle impurity; separate H2 separator loss; exact external stream boundary; input validation and chart scenario rules.
